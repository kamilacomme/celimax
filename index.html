Yes. The formula is pH-balanced and fragrance-free, and Heartleaf extract calms the skin while the BHA works. The acid sits at a low concentration, so it resurfaces without stripping. If your skin reacts easily, start with two nights a week and build up from there.

These pads are made for body skin — arms, legs, back and shoulders — where the barrier is thicker and rough texture builds up. For facial skin, reach for a lower-strength Celimax face exfoliant instead.
